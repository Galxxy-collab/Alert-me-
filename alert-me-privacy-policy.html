Privacy Policy for Alert Me!

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Alert Me! ("the app") is a personal reminder application. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Data Collection

Alert Me! does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies of any kind.

Data Stored on Your Device

All reminders you create — including titles, categories, due dates, and repeat settings — are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not sent to any server operated by us or any third party
  • Is not accessible to us in any form

Notifications

Alert Me! uses Apple's local notification system to remind you of upcoming or overdue tasks. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device via iOS. No notification content is sent to any external server.

Third-Party Services

Alert Me! does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

Children's Privacy

Alert Me! does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "last updated" date.
